Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Toolskit is designed so there is almost nothing to have a privacy policy about. Tools process your data locally in your browser — nothing you type, upload or generate is sent to us. There are no accounts. Analytics and advertising are currently disabled.

Data processed locally

Every tool runs client-side with JavaScript. Text, images, PDFs and files you work with stay on your device for the entire session. The site is served as static files; our servers never see your tool inputs.

Local storage

Two small pieces of data are kept in your browser\u2019s local storage, and only there:

  • Favorites — the tools you star (tk-favorites).
  • Recently used — the last few tools you opened, capped at 8 (tk-recent).
  • Theme preference — light or dark mode (tk-theme).

Nothing else is ever written to storage. Clearing your browser\u2019s site data removes all of it.

Analytics and advertising

Analytics are currently disabled — no tracking scripts load on this site. Advertising is also disabled. If either is enabled in the future, this policy and the cookie policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected.

Third parties

The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which means Google receives a standard font request (your IP address and browser details) when pages load — the same as most websites. No other third-party scripts run by default.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at hello@example.com.

Privacy questions

No. Tools run entirely in your browser, so your input never reaches our servers. We technically cannot see it.

By default, nothing on our side. Your favorited and recently-used tools are stored in your own browser’s local storage and never leave your device.

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. Theme preference, favorites and recent tools live in local storage, not cookies. See the cookie policy for details, including what changes if advertising or analytics are enabled in the future.

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request. To clear what your browser stores locally, use your browser’s "clear site data" option for this site.
