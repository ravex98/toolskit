About Toolskit

Mission. The web is full of simple utilities wrapped in pop-ups, sign-up walls and data harvesting. Toolskit exists to be the opposite: a quiet collection of genuinely useful tools that load fast, respect your privacy and ask for nothing in return.

How the tools work. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. When you count words, compress an image or generate a password, the computation happens on your device with JavaScript — there is no server processing your input, because there is no server involved at all beyond serving these static pages.

Privacy-first by architecture. Privacy here isn\u2019t a promise in a policy document; it\u2019s how the site is built. With no uploads and no accounts, there is simply nothing to leak, sell or breach. The only things stored are your favorites and recently-used tools, kept in your own browser\u2019s local storage where only you can see them.

No account, ever. You will never be asked to sign up, log in or hand over an email address to use a tool. If a feature ever needs your data to leave your device, it will say so clearly before you use it.

Common questions

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no account, no usage limits and no paywalled features. The project is supported by optional donations and, in the future, discreet advertising.

No. There are no accounts on Toolskit at all. Your favorites and recently-used tools are stored only in your own browser’s local storage.

All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text, images, PDFs and files are never uploaded to a server — you can verify this by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads; most tools keep working.

Toolskit is a small independent project. Tools are added and improved over time, and bug reports or suggestions sent via the contact page are genuinely read and appreciated.
