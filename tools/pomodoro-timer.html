Pomodoro Timer

Customizable Pomodoro timer: focus sessions, short/long breaks, and session counter.

Loading tool…

About this tool

The Pomodoro Technique breaks work into focused sprints separated by short breaks, with a longer break every four sessions. This timer automates the whole cycle: it chimes when a phase ends, advances to the next one, and counts your completed focus sessions.

All three durations are configurable, so the classic 25/5/15 is a starting point, not a rule.

How to use

  1. Set your focus, short break, and long break lengths.
  2. Press Start on the Focus phase.
  3. Follow the chimes through breaks; the timer advances automatically.
