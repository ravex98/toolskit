PDF Page Extractor

About this tool

PDF Page Extractor creates a new PDF containing only the pages you pick — useful when you need page 7 of a manual, a single certificate from a bundle, or a custom handout assembled from several sections. List pages and ranges in the order you want them to appear.

Extraction copies pages without re-rendering, preserving fonts, images, and layout perfectly. Everything happens locally in your browser.

How to use

  1. Upload a PDF file.
  2. Enter the pages to extract, e.g. "7" or "2-4,9", in your preferred order.
  3. Click extract to generate the new PDF.
  4. Download the extracted pages.
