Image Contrast Adjuster

Increase or decrease image contrast with a live slider.

About this tool

Image Contrast Adjuster controls the difference between the light and dark areas of your photo. Raising contrast makes flat, hazy images punchier; lowering it softens harsh lighting and can give portraits a gentle, matte look.

Like the brightness tool, changes preview live and apply at full resolution. Contrast pairs well with a small brightness tweak — try both for the best result. All processing stays on your device.

How to use

  1. Upload an image.
  2. Drag the contrast slider and watch the live preview.
  3. Fine-tune until the image has the punch you want.
  4. Download the adjusted image.
